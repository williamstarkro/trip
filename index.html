    Southern king crab, the local specialty. Turkey is unavailable in the southernmost town on Earth. Gratitude is expected to be high.

    Known hazards Beavers

    Introduced in the 1940s for a fur trade that never happened. They have since dammed the last day of the trail. Also: scree, peat, wind.

    The plane Twin Otter, 20 seats

    Punta Arenas to Puerto Williams, 10 kg checked. Flies Monday to Saturday, wind permitting. Wind frequently does not permit.
